## Capture rules

- Use staged or sample data when available.
- Hide athlete names, emails, phone numbers, payment details, face templates,
  access tokens, and infrastructure identifiers.
- Prefer a complete workflow screen with a clear product purpose.
- Record the capture month in the case-study caption.
- Keep a login image only as a secondary image when it adds visual value.
